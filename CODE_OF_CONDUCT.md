# Code of conduct

Heinzelmännchen follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and constructive, assume good intent, critique code and ideas rather than people, and make
everyone — whatever their role, background or experience — feel welcome to contribute.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at helmuthva@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.
