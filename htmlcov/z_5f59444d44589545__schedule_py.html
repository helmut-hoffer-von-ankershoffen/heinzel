1"""When team contracts run: cron slots in the user's time zone, by the daemon's rules (ADR 0020). 

2 

3The daemon (`system.daemon._schedule`) decides when a contract runs; the graph must agree with it, or it invents 

4promises a daemon never makes. Modules don't import each other, so its rules are repeated here; keep them in step: 

5 

6- Cron fields mean wall-clock time in the zone, so slots are computed on naive local times, then placed in the zone. 

7- A wall-clock time that happens twice when the clocks go back runs once, at its first occurrence. 

8- A wall-clock time that doesn't exist when the clocks go forward runs shifted by the gap: 02:30 becomes 03:30. 

9- The zone is `TZ` when set, else the zone file /etc/localtime points at, else the current fixed offset.
