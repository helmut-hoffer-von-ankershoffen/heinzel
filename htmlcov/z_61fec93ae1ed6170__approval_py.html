1"""Scoped approval records and checks for journal-backed effects (ADR 0068). 

2 

3`ApprovalIssuer` is an internal service used by explicit CLI/paired-web confirmation and authorization; issued 

4records are stored privately and never returned to the model or caller. The owning action consumes a proof against 

5its current intent while holding its own journal lock. This records an application-level user decision, not 

6hardware-backed proof of physical presence. CLI and paired web may issue a short-lived decision for a pending 

7schedule action; the MCP chat head consumes that decision from private owner state, without accepting or returning a

182 """Derive the lookup digest for an issued random approval token.
