## [0.9.10](https://github.com/helmut-hoffer-von-ankershoffen/heinzel/releases/tag/v0.9.10) - 2026-11-01

Install and everyday use are smoother. The daemon installs from the app with real progress, an installed app stays signed in after a restart, and an old daemon left behind no longer stops an install. Picking a soundscape plays it right away. Setup offers the four roles and asks for a team name. Adding or removing a plugin no longer upgrades your other plugins, and the app offers Restart and apply when plugins change outside it.

## [0.3.0](https://github.com/helmut-hoffer-von-ankershoffen/heinzel/releases/tag/v0.3.0) - 2026-10-24

A web UI, onboarding, and integrations through the vendors' own CLIs. hz ui opens a local web app: navigation and search, settings, the contract graph, team roster, inventory, doctor and an AI chat. hz join <team repo> recognises you from your gh and Google logins and matches the team roster; hz team creates and manages team repositories, and hz org sync applies the team's recommendations you accept. GitHub, Atlassian, Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Drive, Calendar, Gmail), Zoom (from its summary emails) and mail are first-party plugins that work as an ordinary employee through gh, acli and gws. Plugins bring playbooks with template contracts; contracts form a graph (hz workflow graph) and can look ahead; drafts expire after 90 days. hz system changelog --newer shows what a release brings before you update.

## [0.2.0](https://github.com/helmut-hoffer-von-ankershoffen/heinzel/releases/tag/v0.2.0) - 2026-10-24

Heinzelmännchen now works for you while you're away. A daemon on your machine runs jobs on a schedule and leaves
a receipt for every run; workflow contracts, Markdown files with frontmatter, compile to those jobs. Modules declare
the capabilities they need (`tracker:read`, `documents:read,write`) and configuration binds adapters to them; agent
runners for Claude Code and any command-line agent draft the results. Credentials live in the OS keychain, `hz` tells
you when a newer release exists, and nothing is sent home. Adapters for GitHub and Atlassian ship as plugins.
